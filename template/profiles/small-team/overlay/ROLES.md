# Roles (small team)

Roles are hats, not job titles. One person can wear several.

- **Author**: opens the pull request, whether a human or an agent started it. A human is always named as the owner of the change.
- **Reviewer**: a different person from the author. Reads the diff and the tests.
- **Maintainer**: merges, keeps `AGENTS.md` honest, and decides what to do when a loop goes wrong.
